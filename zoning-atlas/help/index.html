Read a zoning map

Computers misread parts of old zoning maps. You can help by looking at small pieces of them and saying what each one shows. A round is 20 pieces and takes about three minutes, and no account is needed.

We save your answers with a random ID made in this browser. We don't collect your name, email or IP address. Several people read each piece, and a piece is used only when they agree.
